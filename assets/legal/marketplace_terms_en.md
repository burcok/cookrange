# Coach & Marketplace Agreement

Last updated: 29 June 2026

*Draft — applies once paid programs and payouts are enabled. Pending qualified-lawyer review.*

These terms govern coaches, gym owners, and affiliates ("Providers") who sell programs or earn
commissions through Cookrange, in addition to the general Terms of Use.

## 1. Provider Status

Providers are **independent third parties**, not employees, partners, or agents of Cookrange. You are
solely responsible for your services, content, advice, and compliance with applicable law (including
health, consumer, and professional regulations).

## 2. Listings & Content

You warrant that your programs, descriptions, and media are accurate, lawful, your own (or licensed),
and not misleading. Health content must not present medical claims. Cookrange may review, reject, or
remove listings that violate these terms or the law.

## 3. Pricing, Fees & Commission

- You set listing prices within the allowed range. Digital goods sold in-app are processed through the
  Apple App Store / Google Play and are subject to their fees and rules.
- Cookrange may retain a platform commission, disclosed before you publish. Net amounts are calculated
  after store fees, refunds, chargebacks, and taxes.
- Referral/affiliate commissions accrue per the rates shown in the app at the time of the qualifying
  event.

## 4. Payouts

- Payouts (when enabled) are made through our payment provider, subject to identity verification (KYC),
  a minimum threshold, and the provider's terms.
- You must provide accurate payout and tax information. Payouts may be withheld for suspected fraud,
  chargebacks, policy violations, or legal requirements.

## 5. Taxes

You are solely responsible for determining, collecting (where applicable), reporting, and remitting all
taxes arising from your earnings. Cookrange does not provide tax advice.

## 6. Refunds & Chargebacks

Refunds follow the relevant app store and statutory consumer rules. Commissions on refunded or
charged-back transactions are reversed.

## 7. Conduct & Clients

You must treat clients professionally, honour what you advertise, and protect any client data you
access in line with our Privacy Policy and applicable law. Misconduct may lead to removal and
withholding of payouts.

## 8. Suspension & Termination

Cookrange may suspend or remove a Provider for breach, risk, fraud, or legal reasons. Accrued, undisputed
payouts remain payable subject to verification.

## 9. Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Cookrange is not liable for disputes between Providers and
clients, lost earnings, or indirect damages. Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 10. Gym Attribution Commission (QR Invite Program)

This section governs commissions earned through the gym invite-code/QR program (the in-app "Invite
Codes" and "My Earnings" screens), read together with the general pricing, payout, tax, and refund
terms in §3–§6.

- **Mechanism:** A user who signs up through your gym's invite code/QR is **attributed** to you. When
  that user completes a real Premium purchase, a commission accrues to your account — the code
  redemption (sign-up) alone does not generate a commission on its own.
- **Rate & currency:** The commission is a flat amount in **Turkish Lira (₺)**, accruing per the rate
  shown in your gym panel's Earnings screen at the time of each qualifying event (the same "accrue per
  the rates shown in the app" principle as §3) — this document does not fix the current figure; the
  app does.
- **Taxes:** §5 applies unchanged — you alone are responsible for taxes arising from your earnings.
- **Refunds/chargebacks:** §6 applies unchanged — if the attributed user's purchase is refunded or
  charged back, the corresponding commission may be reversed.
- **Payouts:** §4 applies unchanged — **payouts for this program are also processed manually**; no
  automated payout rail exists in this version. Amounts Cookrange owes you may be offset against
  amounts you owe Cookrange (if any, e.g. platform fees).
- **Transparency:** The attributed user's identity is never shown to you — only aggregate counts
  (sign-ups, premium conversions) and your own commission ledger.

## 11. Changes & Contact

We may update these terms; material changes are notified in-app. Questions: **legal@cookrangeapp.com**.
